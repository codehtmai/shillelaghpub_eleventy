## The Hall

Our upstairs hall features vaulted ceilings, great natural light and working rustic fireplace. Hall has its own set of bathrooms, and a separate bar with draft beers available.

It is a comfortable space that works well with both small and large groups, with a maximum capacity of approximately 100 people.

## The Deck

Our covered deck includes heaters and can be fully enclosed during inclement weather.

## Outside Area

We do have an outside area that the club uses for cookouts and other events. There is a blacktop area and also several grassy spots. You can rent these out for your private parties, and yes, our liquor license extends to the entire premises.

## The Main Bar

The Main Bar Room is always available for rent in the mornings and afternoons.

While we generally like to keep the bar open to the public, sometimes we do allow a buy-out of the whole place.

## Parking

We have a very large parking lot with plenty of ample space for your event.

## Food

We have a full kitchen on-site. Outside catering is permitted in our facilities for events for a fee.

## Drinks

We are a liquor licensed facility open to the public with a full bar, craft beer taps, and Guinness on draught!
